# Overview

Costco Wholesale Corporation and its subsidiaries (Costco) operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites, offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1). Founded in 1983 and headquartered in Issaquah, Washington, the company operated 939 warehouses worldwide as of August 30, 2026 (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1). Total revenue for the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, was $303,154,000,000, consisting of $297,247,000,000 in net sales and $5,907,000,000 in membership fees (Source: Earnings Release, Consolidated Statements of Income). Net income for fiscal year 2026 reached $9,226,000,000, or $20.76 per diluted share (Source: Earnings Release, Consolidated Statements of Income). Paid members grew to 84,100,000 in 2026, with total cardholders reaching 150,400,000 (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1).

# Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco operates in a highly competitive global retail and wholesale industry against peers such as Walmart (WMT), Target (TGT), and BJ's Wholesale Club (BJ) (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1). Its membership-driven warehouse club model yields high inventory turnover and customer retention, alongside competitive return on assets relative to peers (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1). For the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, total revenue grew by 10.15% to $303,154,000,000 from $275,235,000,000 in fiscal 2025 (Source: XBRL Facts, Earnings Release). Digitally-enabled sales grew by 20.9% for the fiscal year (Source: Advisor section). Paid members increased from 81,000,000 in 2025 to 84,100,000 in 2026, and executive members represented 42,300,000 of total paid members (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1). Global member renewal rates stood at 89.8%, with the U.S. and Canada renewal rate at 92.3% (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1).

# Valuation Insights

Based on the supplied stock price of $904.70 as of September 23, 2026, and fiscal year 2026 diluted earnings per share of $20.76, Costco trades at a price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of approximately 43.57x (Source: Earnings Release, Price Data, Advisor Section). Costco generated $15,817,000,000 (or $15,825,000,000 per cash flow statement) in net cash provided by operating activities and $9,382,000,000 in code-calculated free cash flow for fiscal year 2026, after accounting for $6,435,000,000 in additions to property and equipment (Source: Financial Performance Analysis, XBRL Facts). The company maintains a strong balance sheet with $89,045,000,000 in total assets, $35,803,000,000 in stockholders' equity, and a current ratio of 1.06 (Source: XBRL Facts, Financial Performance Analysis).

# Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Factors influencing future performance include geographic concentration, with U.S. and Canadian operations representing 86% of net sales and 83% of operating income in 2026, and California alone comprising 26% of U.S. net sales (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1A). Additional risk factors include potential increases in operating expenses such as labor, healthcare, energy, merchandise costs, and potential tariff or foreign exchange fluctuations (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1A; Earnings Release). Strategic execution will rely heavily on maintaining high membership renewal rates, growing executive member penetration, expanding physical warehouses and digital channels, and managing inventory levels (which totaled $19,324,000,000 at the end of fiscal year 2026) (Source: Form 10-K, Item 1; Earnings Release).

# Investment Recommendation

Costco is rated a **Hold** (Source: Advisor Section). While Costco generates exceptional free cash flow ($9,382,000,000 for FY 2026), maintains a solid return on assets (10.36%), and benefits from dominant competitive positioning with high membership retention, its premium valuation (P/E of ~43.57x based on a stock price of $904.70 and diluted EPS of $20.76) fully prices in predictable growth and leaves limited margin for safety against macroeconomic or cost pressures (Source: Advisor Section, Earnings Release).
