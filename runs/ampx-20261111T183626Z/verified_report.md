# Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
Amprius generates revenue through the sale of its advanced silicon anode lithium-ion battery cells and platforms (such as its SiCore product line) to customers in aviation, defense, and advanced mobility sectors.

In terms of competitive positioning, Amprius highlights its technology leadership through high energy density offerings (such as the SiCore 500 delivering 500 Wh/kg on conventional lithium-ion manufacturing equipment) and expanding U.S. manufacturing scale. Market presence is supported by strategic design wins and government contracts, including:
* Securing a $75 million U.S. Department of War competitive contract to produce cells in the U.S. ($22.3 million obligated) to support the conversion of an existing battery production line for an anticipated output of up to 12 million NDAA-compliant cells annually by 2028 (Disclosed in earnings release `EX-99.1`).
