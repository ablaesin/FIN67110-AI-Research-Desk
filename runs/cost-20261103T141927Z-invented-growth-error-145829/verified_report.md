## Overview
Costco Wholesale Corporation operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites, offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally branded and private-label products across a wide range of merchandise categories. The company generates revenue primarily through two streams: selling products at discounted prices via warehouse and digital channels, and collecting annual membership fees from customers (source: Form 10-K). For the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, the company generated total revenue of $303,154,000,000 (`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, source: Form 10-K), representing an increase from the previous period's revenue of $275,235,000,000. Net income for the fiscal year reached $9,226,000,000 (`NetIncomeLoss`, source: Form 10-K; Earnings Release), yielding a diluted EPS of $20.76 (`EarningsPerShareDiluted`, source: Form 10-K / Earnings Release) compared to $18.21 in the previous year. Operating income was reported at $11,685,000,000 (`OperatingIncomeLoss`, source: Form 10-K). 

For the 52-week fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, net sales increased 10.1% to $297.2 billion from $269.9 billion in the prior fiscal year, while fourth-quarter net sales increased 11.2% to $93.9 billion (source: Earnings Release). The fourth quarter was positively impacted by a non-recurring benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from IEEPA tariff refunds received during the quarter, offset in part by a partial reinvestment of those refunds into increased member values (source: Earnings Release). Costco operated 939 warehouses globally as of August 30, 2026 (source: Earnings Release). On the balance sheet, total assets were $89,045,000,000 (`Assets`, source: Form 10-K) against total liabilities of $53,242,000,000 (`Liabilities`, source: Form 10-K), leaving stockholders' equity at $35,803,000,000 (`StockholdersEquity`, source: Form 10-K). Cash provided by operating activities was $15,817,000,000 (`NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities`, source: Form 10-K) and capital expenditures (`PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, source: Form 10-K) were $6,435,000,000, resulting in a positive free cash flow of $9,382,000,000.

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook
Compared to general retail competitors, Costco operates under a differentiated membership warehouse club model focused on high inventory turnover and thin margins. Costco maintains an exceptional competitive moat driven by high membership renewal rates, massive scale, and value creation that continues to drive market share gains across domestic and international regions (source: Earnings Release). 

For the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, annual revenue grew solidly to $303,154,000,000 (`RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, source: Form 10-K), backed by a 10.1% increase in fiscal year net sales and strong digitally-enabled sales growth of 20.9% for the fiscal year (source: Earnings Release). Growth potential is supported by ongoing global warehouse footprint expansion, with payments to acquire property, plant, and equipment totaling $6,435,000,000 (`PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, source: Form 10-K). Key factors that could influence Costco's future performance include consumer and small business spending patterns, domestic and international economic conditions, inflation or deflation, foreign exchange rates, vendor actions, rising operational and employee costs (including health care and wages), energy and commodity costs, tariffs, and geopolitical conditions (source: Earnings Release).
