# Spinoff Worksheet Method — Week 4 Starting Rules

Status: prepared on Nov. 10, 2026. These are starting rules for the first fully documented
spinoff. They are not a completed valuation and are not an investment recommendation.

## Required labels and sources

- Put a source link and a filing section beside every company fact.
- Label every figure `reported`, `historic`, or `pro forma`.
- Do not fill an unknown value with an estimate. Mark it `unknown`.
- Use the first official regular-session close after distribution for the screen price. Record the
  vendor, ticker, date, and whether the series is adjusted or unadjusted.

## Valuation method

For each year, calculate owner cash flow as:

```text
after-tax operating income + depreciation and amortization - capital expenditures
```

- Start with a normalized year only after the information statement and first public filing make
  the business and capital structure clear.
- Keep a low case and a middle case. Do not use only an EBITDA multiple.
- Low case: 0% owner-cash-flow growth, 10% required return, and no terminal-value growth.
- Middle case: 3% owner-cash-flow growth for five years, 9% required return, then 2% terminal
  growth. Use only if the filing facts support those assumptions.
- Show per-share value, the current official close, and the discount or premium to each case.

## Purchase-size and liquidity rule

- Working client order limit: $10,000. This is a project test limit, not a statement about any
  team member's personal portfolio.
- Do not recommend an order if the planned order is more than 10% of the stock's average daily
  dollar volume, or if the filing does not support a reliable share count and capital structure.
- Record the volume source and the date used. If either fact is unknown, leave purchase size as
  `not determined`.

## Conviction rule

| Result | Rule |
|---|---|
| High | Every material worksheet fact has a source; the official close is at or below low-case value; no unresolved parent obligation or liquidity failure exists. |
| Medium | Sources support the main facts, but price is above low-case value or one non-material item is still unresolved. |
| Low / no action | A material fact is unknown, the price exceeds the middle-case value, a transferred obligation is unresolved, or the liquidity rule fails. |

## Mandatory checks before a view

1. Read the information statement and the parent's filing after the distribution.
2. Identify parent obligations that moved to the new company.
3. Check Form 4 filings for open-market purchases after distribution.
4. Compare liquidity with the project order limit.
5. List every remaining unknown beside the conclusion.

Course basis: Greenblatt, *The Big Secret for the Small Investor* (1997), Chapter 3, and
Klarman, *Margin of Safety* (1991), Chapter 10. The numeric assumptions above are transparent
team starting assumptions; neither book is cited as proof that these exact rates are correct.
